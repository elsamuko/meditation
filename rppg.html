Camera heart rate (rPPG) — proof of concept

Sit still in bright, even, non-flickering light facing the camera. Your forehead and cheeks are tracked automatically (green outlines). Give it ~8 s to lock on. Use “Visualize” to see the pulse flush your face. Everything runs locally and offline.
